Notes

Edits land in the show file on Save — a running show keeps its loaded copy until it is reloaded.

Coordinates are floorplan pixels, saved as integers. A polygon keeps at least three points. Rooms may share a wall; they may not share ground — overlaps turn red and are listed here, and the same check runs again on the server at save and at load.
